Feature Story

Business Objective

Understanding how a coffee business performs, product by product, market by market.

This project analyses transactional coffee sales data across products and locations to understand overall sales performance, identify high-performing products, and evaluate regional market trends. The aim is to support business decisions with clear, data-backed insight rather than assumptions.

SQL Server handles data preparation, transformation, and analytical querying. Power BI turns that analysis into an interactive dashboard built for business reporting and ongoing decision-making.

"Which products actually pull their weight?"
Business Questions — In This Issue
01Which products perform best, and which underperform?
02Which markets contribute the most to overall sales?
03How is performance distributed across products and locations?
04Which areas may need further investigation?
