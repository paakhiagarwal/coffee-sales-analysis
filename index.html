Project Overview

Business Objective

Understanding how a coffee business performs across products and regions — and turning that understanding into something decision-makers can act on.

This project analyses transactional coffee sales data across products and locations to understand overall sales performance, identify high-performing products, and evaluate regional market trends. The goal is to support business decisions with clear, data-backed insight rather than assumptions.

SQL Server was used for data preparation, transformation, and analytical querying, while Power BI was used to build an interactive dashboard for business reporting and ongoing decision-making.
